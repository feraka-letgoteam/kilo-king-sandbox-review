# UI references used for the current revision

The goal is comparable composition and interaction clarity while retaining Kilo King's marina, fisherman, cargo, navy and gold art direction. Provider art and game mechanics are not reused.

## Direct observations

Side artwork is scene composition, not a small character squeezed into a header. These captures show the base dock, not an open betting modal or an animation sequence.
- **User recording, Greedy Guineas (8:09 PM):** separate horizontal boost tiles above the bonus cards, balance/base bet together below, and a compact single-bonus confirmation over the same blurred scene. A sampled opening sequence shows a quick reveal with staggered cards. The later entry plaque belongs to the game's illustration language and retains the reel scene behind it. No probability or price from this recording is adopted for Kilo King.
- **User recording, Salad Rush (8:11 PM):** a prominent maximum-feature card, separate spin/chance modifier groups and a lower row of illustrated bonus cards. Window changes expose wrapped prices and a partially clipped lower control strip; the hierarchy is useful, but those fit defects are not targets. Kilo King uses its own available feature set rather than reproducing this menu's offers.
- **Waylander's Forge feature-menu capture:** compact three-column/two-row cards, title above mechanic art, shared bet adjustment and aligned prices/actions. The published gallery is https://burningthedice.com/slots/waylanders-forge/; the provider page is https://valkyriestudio.gg/game/waylanders-forge. The provider page alone does not establish modal geometry.
- **Pragmatic Play, Hot Pepper game overview:** the supplied provider-branded PDF shows the credit/bet/win/Spin hierarchy and describes the denomination selector and sound controls (pages 15–16). - **Nolimit City, Hot Nudge game sheet:** the portrait capture shows a recognizable cabinet, compact monetary readouts and circular controls. It supports the base composition comparison, not conclusions about an unseen buy menu. https://fan-cdn.nolimitcity.com/Nolimit_City_Game_Sheet_Slots_Hot_Nudge_42976_b7b1053089.pdf

## Applied to Kilo King

1. Reserve desktop side columns for the existing title/cargo and full-body fisherman; preserve a clear central reel area and use the compact header on narrower devices.
2. Group the wide dock into readout, bet and spin areas, with Features visibly separate. Keep real amounts readable with tabular numerals and at least 44px interactive targets.
3. Make bet selection a compact amount selector. Make purchase confirmation a focused selected-bonus/cost panel.
4. Structure feature cards as title → illustration → award/price → action, with all cards priced from the same selected base bet. Give Ante its own illustrated activation tile, and keep balance/base bet together below the catalogue. Keep natural triggers and unimplemented previews distinct from available purchases.
5. Use consistent entrance, selection, press and dismissal feedback. The 260ms entrance, 35ms card stagger and 160ms dismissal are our design choices, not measured competitor timings. Reduced motion removes movement and delays.

Competitor sites were not directly operated, and animation durations were not measured precisely.
