# Stage artwork provenance

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for Kilo King, then fitted by code. No reference-provider assets are shipped. The PNG alpha was preserved while converting to WebP; the spool was resized to 256px. The cabinet stays at 1536×1024. Original generation outputs are retained in the session's generated_images directory.

Runtime files:
- `cabinet.webp`: source for twelve independently fitted regions. Hook, wraps and corners keep their proportions.
- `spool.webp`: decorative Spin face, with live icon drawn independently.

The generated artwork is a production input, not evidence of browser validation.

## Cabinet prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production asset for Kilo King, an illustrated night-marina slot game. Generate ONLY a premium hand-painted rectangular marine instrument cabinet surround, isolated with a genuinely transparent background AND a fully transparent empty rectangular center. Wide landscape 3:2 canvas. Straight-on orthographic front view, no perspective. Frame occupies almost the entire canvas with 3% transparent outside margin. Inner opening is exactly a plain rectangular hole, about 87% of canvas width and 82% of height. Four straight rails of even thickness, no symbols or dividers in the opening. Material is worn deep petrol teal enamel (#123f44) on heavy metal with polished warm brass corner guards and dark engraved seams, a few tasteful scratches, tactile illustrated shading matching premium colorful rendered slot symbols. Large clean shapes, deliberate painted edge highlights. Corners have asymmetrical small salt wear and four substantial brass bolts. Two short tan fishing-line wraps over each of the outer vertical corner fittings ONLY, never across the empty center. Top rail has a small engraved abstract fish-hook detail at its center, NOT a crown. Bottom rail is clean and dark, room for game effects, no writing. This is a modern marina smugglers' equipment cabinet, not a pirate treasure chest. Cool blue moonlight on top edges and very restrained warm dock lamp highlights on brass. No neon, magical aura, gems, flames, fancy filigree, rope border, flags, scene, background, UI text, words, numbers, buttons, logos or currency. Strong silhouette and visible illustrated craftsmanship at actual game size; clean game-ready alpha edges. Produce a single complete surround asset, not a mockup or sprite sheet.

## Spool prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Single production UI art asset for Kilo King, a premium illustrated night-marina slot. A circular fishing reel spool used as a game spin button, isolated on genuine transparent background. Orthographic straight-on front face, perfect circle centered, occupying 92% of square canvas. Same tactile materials as a hand-painted dark petrol-teal marine cabinet: narrow substantial worn brass outer rim, two small brass mechanical ears contained WITHIN circle, scratched teal enamel middle ring, large smooth nearly-black navy (#071820) circular center occupying 70 percent of the diameter, completely empty so a sharp white spin-arrow icon can be overlaid in code. Four dark mechanical recesses on the thin middle ring suggest a professional fishing reel. Cool moonlight from top left, small warm highlights, defined cartoon-rendered painterly edges, a believable solid brass lower bevel. Simple, purposeful, carefully authored shapes, not futuristic. Center must stay dark and quiet. NO text, numbers, letters, symbols, arrow, logo, decorative ticks, compass, jewels, gemstones, gears outside circle, rope, glowing aura, background or drop shadow beyond a 2 percent rim. One isolated front-facing round button asset only, no mockup, no multiple buttons.
